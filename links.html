Links

A links page is the most 1996 thing a homepage can have, and also the most useful. I know less than the people below. Go and read them.

Still alive

  • iNaturalist — upload a bad photograph of a brown moth and within a day somebody who knows will tell you what it is. The single best identification resource there has ever been, and free.
  • Atlas of Living Australia — occurrence records for Australian species, which is how you find out whether the thing on your sheet is normal for your postcode or genuinely odd.
  • Bogong moth — start here for the migration, the alpine story, and the collapse in numbers.

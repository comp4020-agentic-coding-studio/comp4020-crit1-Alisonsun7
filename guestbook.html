Guestbook

✉ SIGN THE BOOK ✉

There is no form on this page, and that is deliberate. A guestbook in 1996 was a CGI script that appended to a text file and got overrun by robots within the year. This site runs no script at all, so the book is kept the honest way: you email me, and I type it in by hand, and it appears the next time I update. Slow, small, and impossible to spam.

To sign: send a note to the address at the bottom of the page with the name you want shown. Tell me what you know that I do not.
